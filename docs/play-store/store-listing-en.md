## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
RevLog is a personal vehicle logbook for cars and motorcycles. Keep specifications, maintenance records, and reminders in one place — on your device, without an account.

VEHICLES
• Add multiple cars and motorcycles
• Store details such as license plate, VIN, and more
• Browse and edit vehicle data in a clear layout

SERVICE HISTORY
• Log oil changes, inspections, tires, and other service types
• Track dates, mileage, notes, and costs per entry
• Review maintenance at a glance per vehicle

BACKUPS
• Export selected vehicles to portable .revlog files
• Import backups to restore or merge data on another phone
• Open .revlog files from your file manager or share sheet

REMINDERS
• Schedule maintenance reminders with intervals and lead time
• Get notified when service is due

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Offline-first: your data stays in local storage on your phone
• No cloud sync, no sign-in required
• You control exports and imports

RevLog is built for everyday use: fast, focused, and easy to back up when you switch devices or want a safety copy.

Questions or feedback: see the developer contact on this Play listing.
```
